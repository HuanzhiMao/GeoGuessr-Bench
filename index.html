Findings

Five results from evaluating 23 VLMs; open one to see the data behind it.

Knowing where you are is not the same as finding things

Across 23 models, Precise Localization scores correlate only weakly with Navigation Challenge accuracy (Spearman ρ = 0.39 for both splits). GPT-5.4 is third on Synthetic but ties for 18th on Navigation Challenge.

Each dot is one model; hover or focus a dot for its values.

Show the values as a table

Agents trail the experts, and often fail to commit

The best agent, Claude Opus 4.8, averages 4352.4 points on Competition against the experts’ 4770.3. Pooled over models, 45.7% of Competition and 38.6% of Synthetic episodes score zero, mostly at the step limit or by abstaining.

Share of all episodes, pooled over the 23 models.

Show the counts as a table

Stronger agents tolerate restricted views

With half of each view cut away, Claude Opus 4.8 keeps 98% of its score, Gemini 3.1 Pro only 33%. Across 13 models, the share kept rises with main-setting score (Spearman ρ = 0.73).

Averaged over both halves and both splits; “Main” is the mean main-setting score.

Show the values as a table

Agents rarely inspect the full panorama

Without movement, about two-thirds of episodes with a tool call never tilt the camera. Yet Claude Opus 4.8 scores 4646 on Competition without moving or tilting, covering a median 0.31 of the viewing sphere.

Navigation is the main failure mode

In failed episodes of four models, navigational reasoning failure (FM2) is the most common mode on every task, appearing in 60–80% of them. It accounts for most failures of three of the four models.
